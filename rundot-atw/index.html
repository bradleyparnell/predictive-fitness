Built for Every Race

Whatever distance calls to you,
we've got your back

5K Training

RunDot's 5K training focuses on building speed and VO2 max while monitoring recovery between high-intensity sessions. The algorithm adjusts interval workouts based on how well you're handling the intensity — if you're crushing speed work, it challenges you more. If you're struggling, it builds your base before pushing again.

10K Training

For 10K training, RunDot™ balances speed development with tempo work, adapting the mix based on your performance data. It tracks how you respond to sustained efforts and adjusts tempo runs accordingly. If your lactate threshold is improving quickly, you'll get more challenging sessions.

Half Marathon Training

RunDot's half marathon training adapts long runs and tempo work based on your aerobic development and recovery patterns. RunDot monitors how you handle increasing volume and adjusts your weekly mileage progression accordingly — pace targets always reflect your current fitness, not a generic formula.

Marathon Training

Marathon training with RunDot™ emphasizes aerobic base building while carefully monitoring fatigue accumulation. The system adjusts long run progression based on how well you're recovering and adapting to volume. Training paces evolve as your aerobic capacity improves — always training at the right intensity for today's fitness.

Ultra Marathon Training

For ultra distances, RunDot focuses on time-based training rather than pace, adapting your weekly time on feet based on recovery and life schedule. The system learns how you respond to back-to-back long efforts and adjusts accordingly — fueling and pacing strategies included.
