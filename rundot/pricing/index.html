Frequently Asked Questions

What if I only have limited time to train? ▾
Your time is extremely precious so it's vital that you're spending that time wisely. RunDot is the world's most efficient training platform, allowing you to train up to 30% less time and still reach your goals. RunDot also has a "low volume" training preference setting specifically for those who have limited time.
What level athlete is RunDot most appropriate for? ▾
RunDot is designed for runners of all performance levels, even people who wouldn't consider themselves runners yet. RunDot's algorithms are trained on over a decade of training and race data from first timers to Boston qualifiers, and every level in between.
What's my RunDot® score? ▾
Your RunDot Score is a measure of your performance ability on a 1–100 scale where 100 is world-class performance. Your RunDot Score is "equalized", which means it compares your performance ability against those of a similar age and gender.
What are my TrainX® scores? ▾
Your TrainX scores measure how well you've completed your specific training on RunDot. Your TrainX score is a single number between 1 and 100 that lets you know how you're doing, making it easy to get an instant idea of your progress. It scores individual sessions, weekly training, and multi-week periods.
What is my "threshold" or "FTP" and why should I care? ▾
Your "threshold" or "FTP" refers to your Functional Threshold Pace (or Power if you use a Power footpod like Stryd) and is your primary baseline performance metric. FTP is defined as the maximum pace you can sustain for an all-out, one-hour effort. RunDot automatically determines your threshold from your onboarding data, training, and assessments.
What is EnviroNorm® ("Environment Normalization")? ▾
EnviroNorm® means more accurate analysis, higher quality training, and better race results. Environmental factors such as temperature, humidity, elevation, wind, and terrain have a significant impact on performance. EnviroNorm accounts for these variable factors to keep your training and racing spot on.
