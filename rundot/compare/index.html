THE FOUNDATION

True AI vs template automation

The difference starts at the foundation and cascades through every aspect of your training.

RunDot™

Intelligence built for you

Built on a proprietary AI model trained on over 20 years of real athlete training and performance data. Purpose-built intelligence designed specifically for endurance training optimization. It continuously learns and adapts to you.

Template Apps

Same plan, different runner

Generates template training plans based on a few variables: your general ability level, how many days per week you want to run, and how many weeks until your event. Every athlete of similar ability gets the same plan.

1 ASSESS

Knowing where you really stand

A training plan is only as good as the data behind it.

RunDot™

Comprehensive individual analysis

  • Dot Score™ - a 1-to-100 scale that normalizes for temperature, humidity, and elevation
  • Training Stress Profile quantifies your capacity across aerobic, threshold, muscular, and neural dimensions
  • Adjusts for age, gender, body composition, and historical training response
  • Optional genome analysis through Physiogenomix® technology

Result: your true training capacity, measured and understood

Template Apps

Three questions and a guess

  • How many days per week do you want to run?
  • How many weeks until your race?
  • What is your approximate fitness level?

Result: assumptions from minimal information

2 PRESCRIBE

The right training at the right time

Every workout should serve a purpose specific to your body.

RunDot™

Intelligent, adaptive programming

  • Normalized Training Stress quantifies the exact stimulus for each workout by stress type
  • Adjusts intensities for temperature, humidity, elevation, and indoor/outdoor conditions before each workout
  • Considers residual fatigue and recovery needs when scheduling future sessions
  • One cohesive program for all your planned races

Result: the right stimulus for your body, every single day

Template Apps

Static plans regardless of reality

  • Same paces whether it is 60 or 95 degrees
  • Same progression regardless of how you responded
  • Same recovery assumptions for everyone
  • Single-race templates only - no multi-race coordination

Result: generic plans hoping to fit your situation

3 EVALUATE

Understanding your response

Training quality matters more than training quantity.

RunDot™

Meaningful progress evaluation

  • TrainX® scores evaluate execution quality - not vanity metrics like total hours or distance
  • Higher scores for nailing interval paces, completing prescribed distances, keeping easy runs easy
  • Residual Training Stress quantifies lingering fatigue by stress type for precise recovery planning

Result: actionable insight that optimizes your next workout

Template Apps

Surface-level data tracking

  • Today's weather
  • Workouts planned vs completed this week
  • Distance planned vs completed this week

Result: data you already know, without context

4 PREDICT

Race day, dialed in

Show up knowing exactly where you stand.

RunDot™

Precision race strategy

  • Predicts optimal target paces and race-day splits
  • Factors in local terrain, elevation, temperature, and humidity
  • Built from your current fitness and training adaptations
  • Algorithm continuously improves from real-world race outcomes

Result: a personalized race plan, not a generic pace chart

Template Apps

No predictive capabilities

  • No predicted times based on your actual data
  • No pacing targets based on race-specific conditions
  • No way to learn from outcomes and improve

Result: guessing on the most important day
