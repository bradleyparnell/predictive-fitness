Philosophy

Two very different approaches

RunDot™

Adaptive

Every workout is generated from your real data. As your fitness, fatigue, and life change, your training changes with it, session by session.

Template-based plans

Static

The Difference

01 Onboarding
RunDot™

Cross-sport data analysis and a real performance assessment establish your true starting point before a single workout is written.

Templates

02 Daily Training
RunDot™

Your plan adapts in real time to fatigue, weather, and life. Every session reflects where you actually are today.
