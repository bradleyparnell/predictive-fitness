The Problem

Most training is built for a generic athlete. Yours shouldn't be.

Every triathlete is different. Your fitness, your schedule, your stress levels, your response to training load across swim, bike, and run. None of it matches the person training next to you.

"No pain, no gain." "More volume means better results." These ideas have injured athletes, burned out athletes, and driven people out of the sport they love. And none of them are true.

Backed by 20+ years of athlete training and race data, TriDot® proves that smarter training outperforms harder training every time. You'll train less, reduce your injury risk, and have more energy for the life you're training to enjoy.
