Frequently Asked Questions

What is TriDot?

TriDot is an AI-powered triathlon training platform that builds personalized swim, bike, and run plans using 20+ years of real athlete data and FitLogic™ technology, so triathletes train less, race faster, and reduce overtraining risk.

Do I need a triathlon coach to use TriDot?

No, TriDot's AI-driven training works on its own for athletes at every level. For those who want additional human support, the Premium: Coached plan pairs TriDot's technology with a dedicated certified coach for frequent one-to-one communication.

How is TriDot different from a generic training plan?

Generic training plans are built for an average athlete. TriDot assesses your true performance ability, prescribes the right training at the right time, evaluates how well you executed it, and predicts your race day performance, all personalized to your own data across swim, bike, and run.

Can beginners use TriDot?

Yes. The Lifestyle plan is built for athletes planning one to two shorter-distance races per year, making it a fit for beginners, while more advanced plans support athletes racing multiple distances throughout the year.

What distances does TriDot support?

TriDot supports training across sprint, Olympic, and longer triathlon distances. Plans range from scheduling a single sprint or Olympic race to planning multiple races of any distance, or unlimited races on the Complete and Premium: Coached plans.

What does TriDot's four-phase system include?

TriDot's system has four phases: Assess (identifying your true performance ability), Prescribe (delivering the right training at the right time), Evaluate (measuring training execution and response with TrainX® score), and Predict (RaceX® race day forecasting and pacing).

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Every TriDot plan starts with a 14-day free trial that requires no credit card, and you can cancel anytime.
