Behind the Results

The intelligence behind your leveled time also trains champions.

Meet the engine that makes it all possible.

Your LevelField Time isn't produced by a simple formula or an age-grading table. It's modeled by FitLogic™ - a training intelligence engine built over 20+ years of athlete performance data, developed by the same team behind TriDot®, the Official Training Platform of IRONMAN.

That means when LevelField levels your result, it's using real physics and physiology - not shortcuts. Here's what that actually involves:

1

Age & Gender Normalization

Your result is assessed against an age- and gender-appropriate standard. A 55-year-old woman and a 22-year-old man aren't competing against the same baseline - and neither is their LevelField Time.

2

Environmental Calibration

Race day conditions - temperature, humidity, elevation - are factored into your result. A 4:12 on a hot, hilly course is not the same performance as a 4:12 on a flat, cool morning. LevelField knows the difference.

3

Ranked Against the Entire Field

Once leveled, your result is ranked across the full field - not just within your age group. For the first time, you can see exactly where your performance stands among every athlete who toed the line.
