Behind the Results

Your leveled result wasn't calculated. It was modeled.

The engine behind LevelField Time is built on 20+ years of athlete performance data. Here's how it works.

Your LevelField Time isn't produced by a simple formula or an age-grading table. It's modeled by FitLogic™ - a performance intelligence engine developed by the team behind TriDot®, built specifically to measure and understand human athletic performance at a physiological level.

That means when LevelField levels your result, it's using real physics and real physiology - not shortcuts. Here's what that actually involves:

1

Age & Gender Normalization

Your result is assessed against an age- and gender-appropriate performance standard. A 55-year-old woman and a 22-year-old man aren't competing against the same baseline - and LevelField accounts for that precisely, not approximately.

2

Environmental Calibration

Race day conditions - temperature, humidity, elevation - are factored into your result. A 4:12 on a hot, hilly course is not the same performance as a 4:12 on a flat, cool morning. FitLogic knows the difference, and it shows in your LevelField Time.

3

Ranked Across the Full Field

Once leveled, your result is ranked against every athlete who toed the same line - not just within your age group. For the first time, you can see exactly where your performance sits in the full picture.

FitLogic surfaces what your result actually means - and what's within reach.
