Next Race Planning

The best next race isn't random.
It's the right fit for your goal.

Your history helps you pick smarter, not just sooner.

Picking a race is usually part inspiration, part calendar, part "my friend is doing it." That's fine. But if you have a specific result in mind — a PR, a podium, a personal milestone — the race itself matters more than most people realize.

Course profile, field size, average finish time distribution, historical conditions — all of it plays into what's actually possible on a given day. Here's what to think about before you register:

  • ✓Course profile vs. your strengths. A flat run course rewards run speed. A hilly bike rewards power-to-weight. Match the course to where your data says you're strong.
  • ✓Field depth in your age group. A podium at one race is a top-20 at another. Know the field before you set a rank-based goal.
  • ✓Time of year and conditions. Your last race has a weather and heat baseline. Factor that in before picking a summer race in a different climate.
  • ✓Enough lead time to train. The sweet spot is 3 to 4 months out — long enough to build, short enough to stay motivated.

Your LevelField profile holds your race history. Pull it up before you commit to the next one. A few minutes of comparison can save months of training pointed at the wrong target.
