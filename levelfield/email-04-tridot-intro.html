Your LevelField results are telling you something. Are you listening?

Performance Potential Score

Based on your LevelField Time, FitLogic calculates a normalized performance score - how your result compares to athletes at similar training stages. It's not about where you finished. It's about what your performance signals about where you could go.

Year-Over-Year Tracking

Racing the same event more than once? LevelField tracks your results across seasons and surfaces your Most Improved ranking. A leveled improvement is a true improvement - no tailwind required. Your trajectory matters as much as your time.

Every one of these insights is powered by the same FitLogic engine modeling your leveled result. The picture it builds is more than a leaderboard - it's a genuine measure of your athletic performance over time.
