Race Prediction

Your confidence score is climbing.

Every answer you share with LevelField sharpens your prediction. Here's what drives it.

When you first arrived, your prediction started at low confidence. That's not a flaw - it's the model being honest. LevelField builds from real data, and it takes a few data points before it can say anything meaningful.

The confidence score moves through four stages as the prediction sharpens:

Low confidence
Basic demographics only. A starting point, not a prediction yet.
Building
Training history and recent race results added. The model is getting useful.
Good confidence
Discipline breakdown and recent fitness context in. Prediction is reliable.
High confidence
Full profile built. This is your real race prediction.

What drives your score

The prediction chat collects age, gender, recent race performance, training volume, and discipline history. It's a short conversation and the model updates in real time as you answer.

Most athletes can get to good confidence in a few minutes. High confidence takes a bit more context - but the result is a prediction that genuinely reflects how you're likely to perform on that specific course, on that day.

Why not just guess?

"A prediction is only useful if it can be wrong."

LevelField's confidence score exists because a vague estimate isn't much better than a guess. When your score is high, the prediction has earned the right to be taken seriously.
