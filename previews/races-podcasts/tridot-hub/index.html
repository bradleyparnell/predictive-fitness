Triathlon planning answers

Finding and preparing for an event

How do I choose my next triathlon?

Start with a race format, date and location that fit your goals and available preparation time. Review the organizer details before you commit to registration or travel.

What are the main triathlon race formats?

Sprint and Olympic races are common shorter formats. Middle distance events are often called 70.3, while full distance events are often called 140.6. Organizer naming and distances can vary.

Where does the event information come from?

Dates, locations, formats and registration destinations are checked against organizer-published pages. Event information can change, so confirm it directly before making plans.

Does TriDot organize these events?

No. This is an independent discovery resource. Registration, race operations and final event information remain with each official organizer.
