Race planning answers

Finding and training for a race

How do I choose my next running race?

Start with a date and distance that fit your goals and available training time. Compare locations and organizer details, then confirm registration directly with the official race.

Can RunDot train me for a marathon or half marathon?

RunDot builds personalized run training around your current fitness, schedule and goal race date, then adapts as your training progresses.

Where does the race information come from?

Dates, locations, distances and registration destinations are checked against official organizer pages. Event details can change, so always reconfirm before registering or traveling.

Does RunDot organize these races?

No. RunDot provides an independent discovery resource and personalized training. Registration and final event information remain with each official race organizer.
