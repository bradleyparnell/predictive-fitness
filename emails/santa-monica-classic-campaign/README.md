# Santa Monica Classic x RunDot campaign package

## Delivery sequence

| Send | Timing | Subject | Preview text | CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pre-race | Send 1 | Pre-race | Your Santa Monica plan awaits | Your plan awaits | Start My Plan |
| Pre-race | Send 2 | Pre-race | Race week, your way | Your race week plan | Open My Plan |
| Post-race | Send 1 | Post-race | You did it, Santa Monica | Celebrate your finish | Keep Moving |
| Post-race | Send 2 | Post-race | Your next run starts here | Keep your momentum | Build My Plan |

All dedicated emails use a 1000 × 500 px hero, an eight-words-or-fewer subject line, preview text of 20 characters or fewer, two main copy paragraphs, and a one-to-three-word CTA. The Santa Monica Classic is primary in each header and RunDot is positioned as the supporting Official Digital Training Platform.
