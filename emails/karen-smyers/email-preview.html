Karen Smyers is one of the most remarkable athletes in the history of triathlon. In 1995, she became the only woman ever to win both an Olympic Distance World Championship and an Ironman World Championship in the same year. No one has done it since.

But her story isn't just about titles. It's about longevity, balance, and what it truly means to make this sport a part of your life. Karen is a mother, a coach, and someone who fought back from cancer to keep competing at a high level. She's a living example of what endurance and purpose look like over the long haul.

And this just in: Karen has been announced as a 2026 IRONMAN Hall of Fame inductee alongside Craig Alexander. There couldn't be a better moment to hear from her directly.

Join us this Tuesday for a live conversation you won't want to miss.
 

What Karen Will Share

● What it took to win both world titles in the same year — and why no one has repeated it
● How she balanced elite competition with being a mother and built a coaching career
