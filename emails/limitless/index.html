What Changed Everything

Hi there,

Laura Philipp isn't just one of the fastest women in triathlon history. She's one of the first to train in a way that actually works with a woman's physiology — not against it.

Her numbers are extraordinary: a world-best IRONMAN time of 8:03:27, a women's fastest-ever marathon split of 2:38:27, and the IRONMAN World Championship title on one of the sport's most demanding courses in Nice, France.

But the why behind those results matters more than the results themselves. Guided by the research of Dr. Stacy Sims, Laura rethought everything about how she trained across the course of a month. What followed wasn't incremental improvement — it was record-breaking performance.

We want you to hear it from her directly.
