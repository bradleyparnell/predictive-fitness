Why I Built TriDot

I got into triathlon the same way most people do. One race, completely hooked.

When I started looking for a smarter way to train, I found an industry built on guesswork dressed up as science.

Coaches and platforms claimed to be data-driven. Almost none actually were. They defended philosophies, not tested them. And athletes paid the price: wasted seasons, preventable injuries, results that didn't match the effort.

So I spent 20 years building something better.

Real data. Real athletes. Every theory tested, retested, and validated. What survived became FitLogic, the intelligence engine at the core of TriDot. Not a philosophy. Not a guess. A system built to find what's actually true about training, and apply it to you, individually.
