Why I built TriDot — for you

First, thank you. You chose TriDot for your training, and that's not a decision I take lightly.

I got into triathlon the same way most people do — one race, and I was completely hooked. But when I started looking for a smarter way to train, I found an industry built on guesswork dressed up as science.

"Coaches and platforms claimed to be data-driven. Almost none actually were. They were philosophy-driven — cherry-picking data to defend what they already believed. And athletes like you paid the price. Wasted time. Unnecessary injuries. Results that didn't match the effort."

So I spent 20 years building something better.

Real data. Real athletes. Every theory tested, retested, and validated. What survived became FitLogic — the intelligence engine at the core of TriDot. Not a philosophy. Not a guess. A system built to find what's actually true about training, and apply it to you, individually.
