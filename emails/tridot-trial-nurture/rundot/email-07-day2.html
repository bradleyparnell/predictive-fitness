Your training calendar looks different. Here's why that's a good thing.

If you opened RunDot today, you might notice something different: the paces and distances may not match what you're used to. That's intentional.

RunDot training might be a different approach than you're used to, but each session has a purpose. Every run on your calendar has a pace zone, a duration, and a reason — calibrated to where you actually are right now.

The most common mistake new RunDot runners make: running their easy days too fast. Your training only works when you trust the prescribed paces — especially when they feel slow. That's not a flaw. That's the science.

Under every workout is your RunDot Score — a real-time read on your fitness across aerobic base, speed, and durability. Every workout you complete moves that number. The longer you train, the more accurate and powerful it gets.
