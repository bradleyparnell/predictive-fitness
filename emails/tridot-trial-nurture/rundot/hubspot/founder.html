A Note from Jeff Booher, Founder

I built RunDot because runners who work hard deserve a system that actually works.

{{ contact.firstname }}, I got into endurance sports the same way most people do. One race. Completely hooked.

What I found when I started looking for a smarter way to train was an industry running on guesswork. Coaches and platforms claimed to be data-driven. Almost none actually were. Generic plans. Borrowed paces. Philosophies defended instead of tested.

And runners were paying the price — wasted seasons, preventable injuries, finishing lines that kept moving further away no matter how hard they trained.

"Every runner is an experiment of one. Your training should reflect that — not someone else's average."

So I spent years building something different. Real data. Thousands of real athletes. Every theory tested, retested, and validated. What survived became FitLogic — the intelligence engine at the core of RunDot. Not a philosophy. A system.

The runners I've watched use it well don't just get faster. They train with a confidence they didn't have before. They stop guessing. They stop overtraining. They show up to race day knowing they're ready — because the work they put in was the right work.

That's what I want for you.

Jeff Booher

Founder & CEO, Predictive Fitness | Creator of RunDot & TriDot
