Here's how to make your first week count:

1
Complete your assessment. This is where your RunDot Score gets calculated — your true fitness baseline. This is the most important step because it will define your training intensities and paces.
2
Connect your device. Garmin, Apple Watch, Stryd — whatever you use. RunDot pulls real workout data and keeps refining your training automatically.
3
Run today's workout as prescribed. Even if the pace feels different than what you're used to. Especially then. Trust the data — that's the whole point.
